Who We Are

Tobi's Travel &
Tour Services Inc.

We are a DOT certified Philippine travel agency incorporated as Tobi's Travel & Tour Services Inc. — helping Filipinos and travelers discover the beauty of the Philippines without breaking the bank.

Our name says it all: T.O.B.I. — Travel On Budget Instantly! We believe that amazing travel experiences shouldn't cost a fortune. Since 2011, we've been crafting affordable, memorable journeys for solo travelers, couples, barkadas, families, and corporate groups.

From the pristine lagoons of Palawan to the rolling highlands of Batanes, we've been there, planned it, and helped thousands of travelers experience it — seamlessly.

Meet the Visionary

The woman behind
Tobi's Travels

CT
DOT Certified Travel Manager Licensed Architect · UAP Known as Ms. Lota

Ar. Carlota Suñga-Tiburcio

UAP · DOT Certified Travel Manager

Meet Ar. Carlota Suñga-Tiburcio — or as everyone lovingly calls her, Ms. Lota. She is the heart, mind, and driving force behind Tobi's Travel & Tour Services Inc.

A licensed Architect and proud member of the United Architects of the Philippines (UAP), Ms. Lota brings a unique eye for detail, design, and experience to the world of travel. Her architectural background means she doesn't just plan trips — she designs journeys.

As a DOT Certified Travel Manager, she combines professional expertise with a deep love for the Philippines. Under her leadership, Tobi's Travels has grown from a passion project into a trusted, accredited travel agency serving thousands of Filipinos every year.

Her philosophy is simple: every traveler deserves an extraordinary experience, regardless of budget. That's the Tobi's promise — and Ms. Lota makes sure it's kept, every single trip.
