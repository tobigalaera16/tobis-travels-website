The Cheap Travel Trap

We've all seen it. Someone finds a ₱999 promo fare, books it without thinking, then spends three times the ticket price on airport transfers, overpriced hotels booked last-minute, and tours they could have gotten for half the price through a proper agency. By the end, they've spent more than they would have on a complete package — and had a worse time doing it.

Cheap travel is about cutting corners. Smart travel is about knowing which corners don't matter and which ones do.

🐻
Tobi Bear's Take

"A ₱500 savings on your hotel means nothing if it's an hour away from everything and you spend ₱800 on daily tricycles. Think total cost, not just ticket price."

What Smart Travel Actually Looks Like

Smart travel is a mindset, not a budget level. Here's what it means in practice:

01

Book complete packages, not piecemeal

When you book flights, hotels, and tours separately, you pay retail on everything. When you book through Tobi's, we've already negotiated group rates and bundled deals that you simply can't get on your own. Our packages are cheaper as a whole than the sum of their parts.

02

Travel in shoulder season, not peak

Boracay in December? Beautiful — and ₱8,000 more expensive per person. The same trip in October? Same white sand, 30% fewer crowds, and significantly lower rates. Our team knows exactly when prices dip and how to time your trip for maximum value.

03

Don't sacrifice location for price

The hotel that's "only ₱1,500 a night" but is 45 minutes from the beach isn't a good deal — it's a ₱1,500 room plus two tricycle rides plus wasted time every single day. Smart travelers pay a little more to stay where it matters. We help you find that sweet spot.

04

Know what's worth splurging on

Private island-hopping boat? Worth it. Premium airline seat for a 45-minute domestic flight? Probably not. Tobi's has done hundreds of these trips — we know exactly where your money will feel like money well spent, and where it won't.

05

Use an agency with real accreditation

Booking through a random Facebook page or an unverified travel agent isn't cheaper — it's risky. Tobi's is DOT accredited, PATA member, RTAA member, and TPB affiliated. If something goes wrong, we're accountable. That peace of mind is priceless.

Our founder, Ar. Carlota Suñga-Tiburcio (Ms. Lota) — a licensed architect, DOT certified travel manager, and the woman who has been curating Philippine adventures since 2011 — lives by these principles:

"I've always believed that travel should be transformative, not transactional. When you travel smart, you come home richer in experiences — and still have enough left in your wallet to start planning the next trip. That's the Tobi's promise."

Her top three personal travel rules:

📍
Always stay close to where you want to be.

Distance costs time, and time costs money. A well-located room is always worth a few hundred pesos more.

📋
Plan everything before you leave, enjoy everything when you arrive.

Pre-planned trips are stress-free trips. No scrambling for boats or tours on the day — everything is locked in.

🤝
Always book with someone you trust.
